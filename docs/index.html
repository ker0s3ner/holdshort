Ground control, for real airports.

A ground-operations ATC simulator built on real-world taxiway, runway, and gate geometry pulled straight from OpenStreetMap — with A* taxi routing, a full ground/tower clearance state machine, procedural AI traffic, and voice-driven clearances you speak and hear like a real cab.

Download for macOS View source
Holdshort is a free, hobby-built side project — the macOS build is signed ad-hoc rather than with a paid Apple Developer certificate, so on first launch Gatekeeper will say the app "cannot be verified." Right-click the app → Open (or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway) to run it.

What it does

Real airport geometry

Every taxiway, runway, gate, and apron is converted from live OpenStreetMap data, not a hand-drawn approximation.

Full clearance state machine

Pushback, taxi, hold short, line up and wait, takeoff, landing rollout, gate arrival — every step is a real clearance you issue and the aircraft obeys.

Voice in, voice out

Speak your clearances and have them transcribed locally with whisper.cpp; pilots read them back in natural, NATO-aware synthesized speech.

Unplayed positions automate

Only working Ground? Ramp and Tower keep traffic moving on their own so nothing gets stuck waiting on a controller who isn't there.

Three real views

A Surface Movement Radar plan display, a Tower Cab view rendered over real satellite imagery with a weather-visibility ring, and an Approach radar scope with range rings for vectoring arrivals in.

Procedural, regional traffic

Airlines and routes are drawn from region-aware pools, so you won't see American Airlines spawning in Kaunas.

Coverage

Holdshort currently ships 48 airports across 14 countries, each with real converted surface data. See the coverage map for the full breakdown, or jump straight to the roadmap to see what's being worked on next.

Holdshort is a free, open-source hobby project. Airport surface data is derived from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Tower Cab satellite imagery is served by Esri World Imagery.
